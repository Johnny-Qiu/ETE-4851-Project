Keyboard controls

W forward · S reverse · A left · D right
Combine W+A, W+D, S+A or S+D. Opposing keys cancel.

Click this page before driving.

Releasing throttle keys stops the motors and keeps steering available. A/D continuously adjust steering while held. Releasing A/D holds the last steering position. W/S never changes it. Use Center steering when you want to straighten. A direction reversal pauses at zero for at least 0.5 seconds.

Waiting for encoder readings

Possible-stall detection: reported duty at least 15%, 1 second startup grace, then fewer than 4 counts in 0.5 seconds stops both motors. Also checks reverse motion. Encoder counts are not amps. A disconnected encoder can trigger the same stop. Detection runs on the Pi; it is not electrical overcurrent protection.

Axle setup · change while disarmed

Forward defaults to opposite motor rotation. Check with wheels raised. For a left turn, front wheels point left and rear wheels point right, viewed facing forward travel.

Initial travel is only ±25 µs. Increase in small steps after checking clearance. Pulse limits remain 1000–2000 µs. Settings reset on server restart.
